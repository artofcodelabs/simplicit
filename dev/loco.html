Simplicit + Loco-JS-Model — full CRUD

loco-js-model talks to the server (XHR); simplicit holds the in-memory collection and re-renders. One composed class: class Article extends Reactive(Models.Base).

ReadArticle.get("all") (GET) → Article.load(records)
Createnew Article(attrs).save() (POST) → on success Article.add({ id, ...attrs })
Updaterecord.clone().save() (PUT) → on success record.update(attrs) (memory + re-render)
Deleterecord.delete() (DELETE) → on success record.del() (drop from memory)
